 Mechanism and Applications of CRISPR/Cas-9-Mediated Genome Editing - PMC
Site Author
 Misganaw Asmamaw, Belay Zawdie
Abstract
 This website describes the background of CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing, such as who was involved in its discovery and the experiments that were conducted for its discovery. The site then details the components of Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats (CRISPR) and its associated protein (Cas9). It also describes the mechanism of CRISPR-Cas9 and Double-Stranded Break Repair Mechanisms, which are cellular pathways that repair the double-stranded break caused by Cas9. The site also details the application of CRISPR-Cas9 in gene therapy, gene activation, and gene silencing. It also mentions the therapeutic role of CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing in patients with refractory lung cancer and those with HIV.

 CRISPR–Cas9 Gene Editing: Curing Genetic Diseases by Inherited Epigenetic Modifications - ScienceDirect
Site Author
 Nikhil Deep Kolanu
Abstract
 This site discusses the key findings of different studies that focused on the application of CRISPR-Cas9 in therapeutic settings. It discusses the advantages of CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing and its mechanism. It also details the different applications of CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing in human genetic diseases, cancer research, and infectious diseases. The site also highlights the advancements that have been made on the CRISPR-Cas9 technique. It also mentions the challenges that have arisen when using CRISPR-Cas9. The site describes the ethical considerations regarding the use of CRISPR-Cas9, which include concerns about safety, long-term application, accessibility, and biosecurity. It also talks about possible future routes of CRISPR research to limit risks and produce precise changes in gene editing.
